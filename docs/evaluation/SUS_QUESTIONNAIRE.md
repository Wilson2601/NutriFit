## Scoring

Odd-numbered items: score = answer − 1. Even-numbered items: score = 5 − answer. Add all ten scores and multiply by 2.5 (range 0–100). The app does this for you: `POST /evaluation/sus` (or `app/evaluation.py`). A mean of about 68 is the usual benchmark for average usability.
